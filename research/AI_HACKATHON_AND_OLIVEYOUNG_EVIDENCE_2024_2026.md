# AI Hackathon and Olive Young Evidence, 2024-2026

## Evidence policy

- The primary comparison window is approximately 2024-07-28 through
  2026-07-28. Records whose build period began just before the boundary are
  retained only when their result or technical report falls inside the window.
- A: official organizer/company page with quantitative records.
- B: organizer-owned community/blog page or employer-published job listing.
- C: public winning repository or participant report.
- Inference is labeled separately.
- Missing scores are recorded as unavailable, not estimated.
- Registration/submission ratios are descriptive only because eligibility,
  duration, team size, and counting methods differ.

## What the numbers do and do not prove

- The hackathon records show common winning patterns, not a predicted Cofathon
  score.
- No public organizer page exposes Track 2's score distribution, hidden task,
  or exact criterion weights as of 2026-07-28. Transcribe those values from the
  11:00 briefing rather than inferring them.
- Public registration and submission counts do not prove causal completion
  rates because the populations and rules differ.
- Olive Young production metrics are domain priors, not the hidden test set.
- The four Cofa-Probe axes are the best official preparation proxy, but the
  Cofathon organizer has not published exact weights.
- No source supports a claim that a particular model, framework, or agent count
  will win Track 2.
